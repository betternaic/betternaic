# Privacy

BetterNaic collects as little as possible. Most pages work without sending us anything.

## What we collect

**When you browse:** nothing that identifies you. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies. Your language choice (English or Filipino) is saved in your own browser.

**When you send a report:** the category, barangay and message you write, plus your name and contact details only if you choose to give them. We also keep a one-way hash of your IP address (not the address itself) for 24-hour spam limits.

**Cloudflare Turnstile:** the form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check that you are a person. Cloudflare processes signals from your browser for this check under its own [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## Who can see your report

Only BetterNaic volunteer moderators who sign in with a personal access key. We do not sell or publish reports. We may share the details of a concern (without your contact details unless you agree) with the office that can act on it.

## How long we keep it

Reports are kept while they are useful for follow-up, and deleted after one year, or sooner if you ask.

## Your choices

You can send a report without your name or contact details. To ask us to delete a report, send a new report with the category "Something else" and enough detail for us to find the original.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
