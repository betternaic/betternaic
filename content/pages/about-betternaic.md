# About BetterNaic

BetterNaic is a volunteer-run, open-source portal that puts public information about Naic, Cavite in one place: services and their requirements, officials, barangays, hotlines, history and transparency links.

## Not an official website

BetterNaic is not run by, and does not speak for, the Municipality of Naic. Nothing here replaces an official notice. For transactions, go to the Municipal Hall or the office concerned, and confirm requirements before you travel.

## Part of the BetterGov.ph movement

BetterNaic is listed in the [BetterGov.ph LGU Directory](https://lgu.bettergov.ph), a community of civic portals for Philippine cities and municipalities. It is built on the [Local Government Website Starter Kit](https://github.com/iyanski/betterlocalgov), the open-source template recommended by the directory, and borrows ideas from [BetterIndang](https://betterindang.org), the portal of a neighboring Cavite town.

## Where the information comes from

- Population and barangay counts: Philippine Statistics Authority census results
- Officials: 2025 election results (GMA News, Rappler) and the public record
- History: the Naic article on Wikipedia and the historical marker at Casa Hacienda de Naic
- Requirements: national laws and the websites of the agencies concerned
- Photos: Wikimedia Commons contributors, credited on each page under their licenses

Each page names its sources. If a source is missing or something is wrong, please tell us.

## Security

The report form and moderator sign-in run on Cloudflare: Turnstile keeps bots out, requests are rate-limited, and reports are stored in a private database that only moderators can open. See the privacy page for what we collect.
