BetterNaic is not an emergency service and the report form is not monitored around the clock. In an emergency, call 911.

## Reports

When you send a report, you agree that:

- what you write is true to the best of your knowledge
- you will not post threats, harassment, private information about other people, or false accusations
- moderators may edit, decline, or delete reports, and may relay the substance of a concern to the office that can act on it

Abuse of the form (spam, automated submissions, attacks on the service) is blocked and may be reported.
