# Accessibility

BetterNaic should work for everyone in Naic, including seniors, persons with disability, and people on older phones or slow connections.

## What we do

- Plain language and short pages
- Works on small screens and zooms to 200% without breaking
- Keyboard navigation with a visible focus ring and a "Skip to content" link
- Text and background colors chosen for readable contrast
- Images have text descriptions, and photos are optional to the meaning of a page
- Respects your device's reduced-motion setting
- English and Filipino interface

We aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA.

## At the Municipal Hall

Seniors, persons with disability and pregnant women are entitled to priority lanes in government offices. If you need help with a transaction because of mobility, sight, hearing or any other reason, ask the office in advance.

## Tell us what is hard to use

If something on BetterNaic is hard to read, hear, or use, please [send a report](/report) with the category "Wrong or outdated info on BetterNaic" and describe the problem and the device you used.
