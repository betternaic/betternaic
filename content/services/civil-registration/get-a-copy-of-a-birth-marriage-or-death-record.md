# Get a copy of a birth, marriage or death record

Most offices ask for a PSA copy printed on security paper. For a record registered in Naic, the Municipal Civil Registrar can also issue a certified true copy from the local registry.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Which copy do you need?

- **PSA copy (security paper):** needed for passports, school enrollment, jobs abroad, and most national transactions.
- **Local certified true copy:** from the Municipal Civil Registrar, for records registered in Naic. Useful when the PSA copy is unreadable, has no record yet, or needs to be checked against the local book.

## Who can request

- The person named in the record
- Their spouse, parents, children, or legal guardian
- Anyone with a signed authorization letter and a copy of the owner's valid ID

## Requirements

- [ ] Valid government ID of the person requesting
- [ ] Complete name on the record, date of the event, and place (city or municipality)
- [ ] For birth records: full names of both parents
- [ ] Authorization letter and ID copy, if requesting for someone else

## Steps

1. **PSA copy online:** order through [PSA Serbilis](https://www.psaserbilis.com.ph) and pay online. It is delivered to your address.
2. **PSA copy walk-in:** go to a PSA Civil Registry System (CRS) outlet, fill out the request form, pay, and claim it on the release date given.
3. **Local copy:** go to the Municipal Civil Registrar in the Naic Municipal Hall, fill out the request form, pay at the Municipal Treasurer, and claim the copy.

## Fees and processing time

PSA fees are set by the PSA, and delivered copies cost more than walk-in copies. See [psa.gov.ph](https://psa.gov.ph) for current rates. Local certified copies follow the municipal fee ordinance.
