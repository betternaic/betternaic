# Apply for a marriage license

Couples apply together at the civil registrar of the city or municipality where either of them lives. The license is released after a 10-day posting period and is valid anywhere in the Philippines for 120 days from release.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can apply

Both parties, appearing in person, at the Municipal Civil Registrar of Naic if either of you lives here.

## Requirements

- [ ] Accomplished marriage license application, signed by both
- [ ] PSA birth certificate of each applicant
- [ ] PSA Certificate of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR) of each applicant
- [ ] Valid government IDs
- [ ] Certificate of attendance at the pre-marriage orientation and counseling required by the municipality
- [ ] Ages 18 to 21: written consent of a parent or guardian
- [ ] Ages 21 to 25: written advice of a parent or guardian
- [ ] Previously married: death certificate of the former spouse, or the court decree of annulment or nullity with the annotated marriage certificate
- [ ] Foreign national: certificate of legal capacity to marry from their embassy

## Steps

1. Get the application form and the schedule of the pre-marriage orientation from the Municipal Civil Registrar.
2. Attend the orientation and counseling together.
3. File the application with all documents and pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer.
4. Wait out the 10-day posting period.
5. Claim the license and give it to your solemnizing officer before the wedding.

## Fees and processing time

Fees follow the municipal fee ordinance. Counting the posting period, plan for at least two weeks.
