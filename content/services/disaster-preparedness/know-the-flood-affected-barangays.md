# Know the flood-affected barangays

After the July 2018 floods, twelve Naic barangays were placed under a state of calamity. This list is background for planning, not a current hazard map.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

Source: [ABS-CBN News, July 22, 2018](https://abs-cbn.com/news/07/22/18/more-areas-placed-under-state-of-calamity-due-to-floods).

## What this list does not tell you

- It does not mean other barangays are safe. Floods change as subdivisions, roads and drainage are built.
- It is not an official hazard map. For your exact address, check [HazardHunterPH](https://hazardhunter.georisk.gov.ph), and ask the MDRRMO for the municipality's current hazard maps.

## If you live in one of these barangays

1. Know your evacuation center and route before the rainy season.
2. Keep a go-bag ready from June to November.
3. Move early when your barangay calls for evacuation, especially if you have seniors, children, PWDs or pregnant family members.
