# Get a zoning or locational clearance

A zoning or locational clearance confirms that what you plan to build or run fits the land use allowed in that area under Naic's zoning ordinance. It comes before the building permit, and some businesses need it for their Mayor's permit.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Requirements

- [ ] Application form from the Municipal Planning and Development Office (MPDO)
- [ ] Proof of ownership (title or tax declaration), or a contract of lease or consent of the owner
- [ ] Lot plan or vicinity map showing where the property is
- [ ] Site development plan or building plans, for construction
- [ ] Barangay clearance
- [ ] Other clearances, depending on the project (for example, an environmental compliance certificate for some industries)

## Steps

1. Ask the MPDO, also called the Zoning Office, what zone your lot is in and what is allowed there.
2. File the application and documents.
3. The office evaluates the application and may inspect the site.
4. Pay the fee at the Municipal Treasurer.
5. Claim the clearance.

If your project is not allowed in the zone, the office will explain your options, such as a variance or exception under the zoning ordinance.

## Fees

Fees follow the municipal zoning ordinance and revenue code.
