# Get an occupancy permit

A finished building needs a Certificate of Occupancy before anyone may use or live in it. Businesses also need it for their permit.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Requirements

- [ ] Application for a Certificate of Occupancy
- [ ] Copy of the approved building permit and its ancillary permits
- [ ] Certificate of Completion, signed by the owner and the professional who supervised the work
- [ ] As-built plans, if the construction changed from the approved plans
- [ ] Construction logbook or daily record, if the office asks for it
- [ ] Fire Safety Inspection Certificate for occupancy from the Bureau of Fire Protection
- [ ] Photos of the finished building

## Steps

1. File the application at the Office of the Building Official in the Naic Municipal Hall.
2. The office inspects the building against the approved plans.
3. Pay the assessed fees at the Municipal Treasurer.
4. Claim the Certificate of Occupancy.

## Fees

Fees follow the National Building Code schedule and local ordinances.
