# Get a building permit

You need a building permit before you build, add to, renovate or demolish a structure in Naic. The Office of the Building Official (under the Municipal Engineering Office) issues it under the National Building Code (PD 1096).

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Requirements

- [ ] Unified Application Form for the building permit, with the electrical, sanitary or plumbing, and mechanical permits that apply
- [ ] Proof of land ownership (title or tax declaration), or a contract of lease or notarized consent of the owner
- [ ] Locational or zoning clearance from the Municipal Planning and Development Office
- [ ] Barangay clearance for the construction
- [ ] Sets of plans signed and sealed by licensed professionals: architectural, structural, electrical, plumbing or sanitary, and mechanical as applicable (ask how many sets)
- [ ] Bill of materials, cost estimate and specifications
- [ ] Structural design analysis, when required by the size of the building
- [ ] Fire Safety Evaluation Clearance from the Bureau of Fire Protection
- [ ] PRC IDs and professional tax receipts of the signing professionals

## Steps

1. Secure the zoning or locational clearance from the MPDO.
2. Have your plans prepared, signed and sealed by licensed professionals.
3. File the application and documents at the Office of the Building Official in the Naic Municipal Hall.
4. Wait for the plan evaluation and the assessment of fees.
5. Pay at the Municipal Treasurer.
6. Claim the permit and post it at the construction site.
7. Allow the required inspections during construction, then apply for an occupancy permit when you finish.

## Fees and processing time

Fees are computed under the National Building Code schedule and local ordinances, based mostly on floor area and use. The Ease of Doing Business Act (RA 11032) sets maximum processing times.
