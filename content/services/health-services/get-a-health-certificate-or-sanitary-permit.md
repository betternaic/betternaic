# Get a health certificate or sanitary permit

Under the Code on Sanitation (PD 856), food handlers and workers in food and similar businesses need a health certificate, and the business itself needs a sanitary permit. Both come from the Municipal Health Office and are renewed every year.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Health certificate (for workers)

- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Recent 1x1 photo
- [ ] Results of the medical tests the health office requires (often a physical exam, and tests such as a stool exam or chest x-ray depending on the work)
- [ ] Attendance at a food safety seminar, if required

## Sanitary permit (for the business)

- [ ] Application form
- [ ] Business permit application or previous permit
- [ ] Health certificates of all food handlers and workers
- [ ] Water analysis or proof of safe water source, if required
- [ ] Pest control and waste disposal arrangements

## Steps

1. Ask the Municipal Health Office at the Naic Municipal Hall for the current requirements and schedule of examinations.
2. Complete the medical tests and seminar.
3. For the sanitary permit, a sanitary inspector inspects your establishment.
4. Pay the fees at the Municipal Treasurer.
5. Claim the certificate or permit and display it as required.

## Fees

Fees follow the municipal revenue code.
