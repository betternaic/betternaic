# Register for PhilHealth YAKAP

YAKAP (Yaman ng Kalusugan Program) is PhilHealth's primary care benefit, formerly called Konsulta. Once you register with an accredited YAKAP clinic, you get free consultations, selected laboratory tests, and medicines there.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can register

Every Filipino is a PhilHealth member under the Universal Health Care Act (RA 11223), so you can register even if you have never paid contributions. Each family member registers individually.

## Requirements

- [ ] PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN). If you do not have one, PhilHealth can give you one.
- [ ] Valid ID

## Steps

1. Choose one YAKAP-accredited clinic near you. Ask the Naic Rural Health Unit if it is accredited, or look up accredited providers on the [PhilHealth website](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph).
2. Register with that clinic through the PhilHealth Member Portal, the eGovPH app, or in person at the clinic.
3. Go for your first consultation. The clinic will do a health profile and may schedule laboratory tests.
4. Get your prescribed medicines from the clinic or its partner pharmacy, as covered by the program.

You can only be registered with one YAKAP clinic at a time. Use the same clinic for your check-ups.

## Fees

Covered consultations, tests and medicines are free at your YAKAP clinic.
