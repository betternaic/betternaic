# See a doctor at the Rural Health Unit

The Naic Rural Health Unit (Municipal Health Office) and the barangay health stations offer free primary care: consultations, maternal care, immunization, TB treatment, family planning, and health programs.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Services you can ask for

- Consultations and basic treatment for common illnesses
- Prenatal and postnatal check-ups
- Childhood immunization and seasonal vaccine drives
- TB testing and treatment (TB-DOTS)
- Family planning counseling and methods
- Blood pressure and blood sugar monitoring
- Referrals to hospitals when you need specialist care

## What to bring

- [ ] Valid ID or barangay certificate
- [ ] PhilHealth Identification Number (PIN), if you have one
- [ ] Previous prescriptions, test results or your health record booklet
- [ ] For children and pregnant women: the Mother and Child Book or immunization card

## Steps

1. Go early. Patients are usually seen first come, first served, with priority for seniors, PWDs and pregnant women.
2. Register at the front desk.
3. Wait for your consultation.
4. Get your prescription, free medicines if available, or a referral.

## Where to go

The Naic Rural Health Unit is listed in public directories on Capt. C. Nazareno St., Poblacion. Your barangay health station can handle many check-ups closer to home.

## Fees

Consultations at the RHU are free. If the RHU is accredited for PhilHealth YAKAP, registering there unlocks more free tests and medicines.
