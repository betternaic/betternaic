# Pay real property tax

Owners of land, buildings and machinery pay real property tax (amilyar) every year to the Municipal Treasurer. You can pay in full or in four quarterly installments.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Deadlines

Under the Local Government Code, the four installments are due on or before:

- March 31
- June 30
- September 30
- December 31

The municipality may grant a discount for paying in advance or on time. Unpaid tax earns interest of 2% a month, for up to 36 months.

## Requirements

- [ ] Tax declaration number or your last official receipt
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Authorization letter and ID copy, if paying for the owner

## Steps

1. Go to the Municipal Treasurer in the Naic Municipal Hall, or ask about other payment channels the municipality accepts.
2. Give your tax declaration number. The cashier computes what you owe, including any arrears.
3. Pay and keep the official receipt. You will need it to get a tax clearance when you sell, transfer or mortgage the property.

## How the tax is computed

The tax is a percentage of the assessed value shown on your tax declaration, plus the Special Education Fund levy. The Municipal Assessor sets the assessed value; the Treasurer collects. If the assessment looks wrong, talk to the Assessor first.
