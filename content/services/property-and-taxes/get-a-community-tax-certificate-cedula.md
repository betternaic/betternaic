# Get a community tax certificate (cedula)

The community tax certificate, or cedula, is often asked for in permits, notarized documents and job applications. Residents 18 and older who earn income or own property must pay it each year, before the end of February.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who pays

- Residents 18 and older who are employed, run a business, or own real property worth more than the amount set by law
- Corporations and businesses operating in Naic

## How much

The Local Government Code sets the amount:

- **Individuals:** ₱5 basic tax, plus ₱1 for every ₱1,000 of gross income from business, work or property, up to ₱5,000 in additional tax
- **Corporations:** ₱500 basic tax, plus ₱2 for every ₱5,000 of assessed property value or gross receipts, up to ₱10,000 in additional tax

Payment after the last day of February adds interest.

## Requirements

- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Your income for the previous year (for example, your income tax return or certificate of compensation), to compute the additional tax

## Steps

1. Go to the Municipal Treasurer in the Naic Municipal Hall. Some barangays also issue cedulas; ask your barangay hall.
2. Give your details and income.
3. Pay and get your cedula.
