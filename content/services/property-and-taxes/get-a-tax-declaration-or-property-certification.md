# Get a tax declaration or property certification

The Municipal Assessor keeps the tax declarations for land and buildings in Naic. You can ask for certified copies, transfer a tax declaration after a sale, or get certifications such as landholding or no-improvement.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Common requests

- Certified true copy of a tax declaration
- Certification of landholding, or of no landholding
- Certification of no improvement (vacant lot)
- New declaration for a new building
- Transfer of a tax declaration to a new owner

## Requirements

- [ ] Request form from the Assessor's office
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Proof of ownership (title or deed), or an authorization letter or special power of attorney from the owner
- [ ] Real property tax clearance or latest official receipt, when asked

For a transfer after a sale, the Assessor usually also needs the deed of sale, the BIR electronic Certificate Authorizing Registration (eCAR), the transfer tax receipt, and the new title if already issued. Ask for the full list before you go.

## Steps

1. Go to the Municipal Assessor in the Naic Municipal Hall and fill out the request form.
2. Pay the certification fee at the Municipal Treasurer.
3. Claim your document on the release date given.

## Fees

Certification fees follow the municipal revenue code.
