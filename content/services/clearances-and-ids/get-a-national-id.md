# Get a National ID

The Philippine Identification System (PhilSys), created by RA 11055, gives every Filipino and resident alien a free National ID. It is accepted as proof of identity in government and private transactions.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can register

Filipino citizens and resident aliens of any age, including children.

## Requirements

Bring one of these:

- [ ] PSA birth certificate plus one valid government ID with your photo
- [ ] A Philippine passport or ePassport
- [ ] A UMID card

If you have none of these, ask the registration center which other documents are accepted.

## Steps

1. Find a PhilSys registration center or schedule. Check [philsys.gov.ph](https://philsys.gov.ph), or ask the Municipal Hall or your barangay about registration days in Naic.
2. Go to the center with your documents.
3. Give your personal details and have your photo, fingerprints and iris scan taken.
4. Keep the transaction slip.
5. Get your ID: the printed card is delivered, and you can also use the ePhilID or the digital National ID in the eGovPH app while you wait.

## Fees

Registration and the ID are free.
