# Get a police clearance

The national police clearance is applied for online, then completed at a police station of your choice where your photo and fingerprints are taken.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Requirements

- [ ] An account on the PNP National Police Clearance System: [pnpclearance.ph](https://pnpclearance.ph)
- [ ] Two valid government IDs (bring the originals)
- [ ] Reference number and proof of payment
- [ ] For first-time jobseekers: your barangay certification under RA 11261, to have the fee waived

## Steps

1. Register at [pnpclearance.ph](https://pnpclearance.ph) and fill out your details.
2. Choose the police station where you will appear (for example, the station serving Naic) and book a date.
3. Pay the fee through one of the online or over-the-counter payment channels shown.
4. Go to the station on your appointment date with your IDs and reference number.
5. Have your photo, fingerprints and signature taken.
6. Claim your clearance. If you have a namesake with a record, verification can take longer.

## Fees

The fee is shown during booking. First-time jobseekers get it free once.
