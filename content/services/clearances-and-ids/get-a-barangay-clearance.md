# Get a barangay clearance

A barangay clearance shows that you live in the barangay and have no pending case there. It is asked for in job applications, business permits, and many other transactions.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can apply

Residents of the barangay. Apply at the barangay hall where you live. For a business clearance, apply where the business is located.

## Requirements

- [ ] Valid ID showing your address, or proof that you live in the barangay
- [ ] Community tax certificate (cedula), if the barangay asks for it
- [ ] Purpose of the clearance (for example, employment, business, or bank)
- [ ] For first-time jobseekers: say so, to get it free under RA 11261

## Steps

1. Go to your barangay hall during office hours.
2. Fill out the request form and state the purpose.
3. Pay the fee at the barangay treasurer, if any.
4. Wait for the clearance to be signed by the Punong Barangay. It is often released the same day.

## Fees

Barangay fees are set by each barangay's ordinance. First-time jobseekers pay nothing.
