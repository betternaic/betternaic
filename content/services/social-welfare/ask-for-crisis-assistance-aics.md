# Ask for crisis assistance (AICS)

Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations (AICS) helps with medical, burial, transportation, education or food needs during an emergency. It is a DSWD program that the MSWDO also helps residents apply for.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can apply

Residents facing a crisis they cannot cover on their own, as assessed by a social worker.

## Requirements

- [ ] Valid ID of the client
- [ ] Barangay certificate of indigency or residency
- [ ] Documents for the specific need:
  - Medical: medical certificate or clinical abstract signed by the doctor (with license number), plus the hospital bill, prescription or quotation
  - Burial: death certificate and funeral contract
  - Education: school ID and certificate of enrollment or statement of account
  - Transportation: proof of the travel need
- [ ] Any other document the social worker asks for after the interview

## Steps

1. Get a barangay certificate of indigency from your barangay hall.
2. Go to the MSWDO at the Naic Municipal Hall, or to a DSWD Field Office or satellite office.
3. Fill out the intake form and talk to a social worker.
4. The social worker assesses your case and the type and amount of help.
5. Receive the assistance, in cash or as a guarantee letter to a hospital, pharmacy or funeral parlor.

## Fees

Applying is free. Do not pay anyone who offers to speed it up.
