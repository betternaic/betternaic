# Get a PWD ID

Persons with disability who live in Naic can get a PWD ID through the Persons with Disability Affairs Office (PDAO) or the MSWDO. The ID gives you the discounts and privileges of the Magna Carta for Persons with Disability (RA 7277, as amended).

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can apply

Residents of any age with a long-term physical, mental, intellectual or sensory disability, including chronic illness that limits daily life.

## Requirements

- [ ] Accomplished PWD registration form (Philippine Registry for Persons with Disability)
- [ ] Medical certificate or clinical abstract from a licensed physician stating the disability (for disabilities that cannot be seen)
- [ ] Proof of residency in Naic, such as a barangay certificate
- [ ] Recent 1x1 photos (ask how many)
- [ ] Valid ID or birth certificate of the applicant
- [ ] For minors: valid ID of the parent or guardian

## Steps

1. Get the medical certificate, if needed, and a barangay certificate.
2. Go to the PDAO or MSWDO at the Naic Municipal Hall.
3. Submit the form and documents. Your data is entered in the national PWD registry.
4. Claim your ID on the date given.

## What the ID gets you

- 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicines, medical services, transport fares, restaurants, and other items covered by law
- Priority lanes and express service
- Automatic PhilHealth coverage as a PWD member

## Validity and fees

The ID is free. PWD IDs are valid for a set number of years and must be renewed; ask the office when yours expires.
