# Get a senior citizen ID

Residents aged 60 and above can get a senior citizen ID from the Office of the Senior Citizens Affairs (OSCA). The ID gives you the discounts and privileges of the Expanded Senior Citizens Act (RA 9994).

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can apply

Filipino citizens aged 60 and above who live in Naic.

## Requirements

- [ ] Proof of age: PSA birth certificate, or a valid government ID showing your birth date
- [ ] Proof of residency in Naic, such as a barangay certificate
- [ ] Recent 1x1 or 2x2 photos (ask how many)
- [ ] Accomplished OSCA application form

## Steps

1. Get a barangay certificate of residency from your barangay hall.
2. Go to the OSCA or the Municipal Social Welfare and Development Office (MSWDO) at the Naic Municipal Hall.
3. Submit the form and documents.
4. Claim your ID and purchase booklets on the date given.

## What the ID gets you

- 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicines, medical and dental services, transport fares, restaurants, and other items covered by RA 9994
- Priority lanes in government and private offices
- Purchase booklets for medicines and basic goods

## Other benefits to ask about

- **Social pension:** monthly cash support for indigent seniors under DSWD, applied for through the MSWDO
- **Cash gifts:** under the Expanded Centenarians Act (RA 11982), seniors receive cash gifts at ages 80, 85, 90 and 95, and a larger gift at 100

## Fees

The ID is free.
