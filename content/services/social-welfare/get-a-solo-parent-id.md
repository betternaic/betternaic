# Get a solo parent ID

Solo parents who live in Naic can get a solo parent ID from the MSWDO under the Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act (RA 11861). The ID is renewed every year.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who counts as a solo parent

RA 11861 covers, among others, parents raising a child alone because the other parent died, is detained, has a long-term incapacity, left, or is separated by annulment or legal separation; unmarried parents who keep and raise the child; spouses or family members of OFWs in some cases; and guardians who take on the role of the parent.

## Requirements

- [ ] Accomplished application form from the MSWDO
- [ ] PSA birth certificate of each child
- [ ] Barangay certificate showing your residency in Naic and your solo parent status
- [ ] Proof of the circumstance that makes you a solo parent, for example a death certificate, a court decision, a certificate of detention, a medical certificate, or a sworn statement of abandonment
- [ ] Proof of income (such as an income tax return or certificate of indigency), for benefits that depend on income
- [ ] Recent 1x1 photos (ask how many)

## Steps

1. Get a barangay certificate from your barangay hall.
2. Go to the MSWDO at the Naic Municipal Hall.
3. Submit the form and documents. A social worker may interview you.
4. Claim your ID. Renew it every year.

## Benefits to ask about

Depending on your income and work, benefits include up to seven days of paid parental leave a year for employees, flexible work arrangements, priority in housing and scholarship programs, discounts on some items for young children, and a monthly cash subsidy for low-income solo parents.

## Fees

The ID is free.
