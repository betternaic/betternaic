# Report illegal dumping or burning

Dumping garbage in canals, vacant lots or the sea, and burning garbage, are not allowed under RA 9003. Report them to the barangay first, since barangays handle collection and enforcement closest to you.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## What to note down

- [ ] Exact place (street, landmark, purok and barangay)
- [ ] Date and time you saw it
- [ ] What was dumped or burned, and how often it happens
- [ ] Photos, if you can take them safely
- [ ] Vehicle plate number, if a vehicle was used

## Where to report

1. **Your barangay hall:** the barangay can act on the spot and has its own anti-littering rules.
2. **Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO):** for repeat cases, large dumps, or if the barangay does not act.
3. **DENR Environmental Management Bureau:** for industrial waste or serious pollution.

You can also send the details through the BetterNaic [report form](/report). BetterNaic is not the LGU, so it cannot enforce anything; volunteers review reports and point them to the right office.

## Stay safe

Do not confront people who are dumping or burning. Do not breathe in smoke from burning plastic or chemicals.
