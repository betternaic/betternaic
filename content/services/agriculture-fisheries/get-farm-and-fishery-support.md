# Get farm and fishery support

Naic still has rice fields inland and fishing communities along Manila Bay. The Municipal Agriculture Office is the local door to Department of Agriculture and BFAR programs for both.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Programs to ask about

- **Seeds and fertilizer** distributed through DA programs, mostly to RSBSA-registered farmers
- **Crop, livestock and fishery insurance** from the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation (PCIC), which pays out after calamities and pests
- **Loans** for farmers and fisherfolk through the Agricultural Credit Policy Council and partner lenders
- **Fishing gear, boats and livelihood support** through BFAR
- **Training** on farming, aquaculture and food processing, often with the Agricultural Training Institute or TESDA
- **Technical help** from agricultural technicians for crop pests, diseases and livestock

## Requirements

- [ ] RSBSA registration (most programs require it)
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Program-specific documents, which the Municipal Agriculture Office will list

## Steps

1. Register in the RSBSA if you have not yet.
2. Ask the Municipal Agriculture Office which programs are open and when.
3. Submit the requirements for the program you want.
4. Watch for announcements on distribution dates.

## Fees

DA and BFAR assistance programs are free. Insurance and loans have their own terms.
