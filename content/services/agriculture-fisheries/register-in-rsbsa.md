# Register in the RSBSA

The Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) is the Department of Agriculture's list of farmers, farm workers and fisherfolk. Being in the RSBSA is usually required before you can receive DA programs such as seeds, fertilizer, cash assistance, crop insurance and loans.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can register

Farmers, farm workers, fisherfolk, and agri-youth who live or work in Naic.

## Requirements

- [ ] Accomplished RSBSA enrollment form
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Recent photo (ask what size)
- [ ] For farmers: details of the farm, and proof of land tenure if you have it (title, tax declaration, CLOA, or lease or tenancy agreement)

## Steps

1. Go to the Municipal Agriculture Office at the Naic Municipal Hall, or ask your barangay when the agricultural technician will be enrolling.
2. Fill out the form with the technician's help.
3. Your farm or fishing details are checked and encoded.
4. Ask for your RSBSA reference number and keep it.

Fisherfolk should also ask about registration in the BFAR fisherfolk registry (FishR) and the registration of small fishing boats (BoatR).

## Fees

Registration is free.
