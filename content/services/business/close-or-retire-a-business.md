# Close or retire a business

If you stop operating, retire the business with the BPLO so you are not billed taxes and penalties for the following years. Closing with the BIR is a separate process.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Requirements

- [ ] Application or letter for retirement of business
- [ ] Sworn declaration of gross sales or receipts for the current year, up to the date of closure
- [ ] Latest business permit and official receipts
- [ ] Barangay certification that the business has closed
- [ ] Valid ID, and an authorization letter if a representative files

## Steps

1. Get a barangay certification of closure.
2. File the retirement application at the BPLO in the Naic Municipal Hall.
3. The municipality may inspect to confirm the business has stopped.
4. Pay any tax due on your gross sales for the year of closure. The Local Government Code requires this before retirement.
5. Claim your certificate of retirement.
6. Close your registration with the [BIR](https://www.bir.gov.ph) (BIR Form 1905) and surrender unused receipts and invoices.

## Fees

Taxes due depend on your gross sales for the year. Other fees follow the municipal revenue code.
