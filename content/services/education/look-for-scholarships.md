# Look for scholarships

Most scholarships for Naic students come from national agencies, plus programs the municipality or the Sangguniang Kabataan may open each year.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## National programs

- **Free tuition at state universities (RA 10931):** qualified students at state universities and colleges, such as Cavite State University, pay no tuition.
- **CHED scholarships and grants:** merit scholarships and financial assistance for college students. See [ched.gov.ph](https://ched.gov.ph).
- **UniFAST Tertiary Education Subsidy (TES):** cash grants for students from low-income families in public and private colleges. See [unifast.gov.ph](https://unifast.gov.ph).
- **DOST-SEI scholarships:** for students taking science, technology, engineering and math courses. See [sei.dost.gov.ph](https://sei.dost.gov.ph).
- **TESDA scholarships:** free technical-vocational training, often with a training allowance. See [tesda.gov.ph](https://www.tesda.gov.ph).

## Local programs

Ask the Office of the Municipal Mayor, the MSWDO, or your Sangguniang Kabataan whether a municipal or barangay scholarship is open this year, and when applications start.

## Common requirements

- [ ] Application form
- [ ] PSA birth certificate
- [ ] Report card, transcript or certificate of grades
- [ ] Certificate of enrollment or registration
- [ ] Proof of family income (income tax return or certificate of indigency)
- [ ] Barangay certificate of residency

## Tips

- Apply early. Most programs open once a year and close quickly.
- Keep copies of everything you submit.
- Scholarships are free to apply for. Do not pay anyone who promises to get you in.
