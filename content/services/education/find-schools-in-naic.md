# Find schools in Naic

Public basic education in Naic is handled by DepEd through three school district offices: Naic I, Naic II and Naic III. For college, Cavite State University has a campus in Naic.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Public elementary schools

Public elementary schools listed for Naic include those in Balsahan, Bancaan, Bucana, Bucana Sasahan, Calubcob, Ciudad Nuevo, Gombalza (Poblacion), Halang, Humbac, Ibayo Estacion, Ibayo Silangan, Kanluran Rizal, Labac, Latoria, Mabolo, Makina, Malainen Bago, Malainen Luma, Molino, Munting Mapino, Muzon, Palangue, Sabang, San Roque, Santulan, Sapa, Timalan Balsahan and Timalan Concepcion. Naic Elementary School occupies the historic Casa Hacienda de Naic.

For the school nearest you and for high schools, ask at your barangay hall or the district office, or check the [DepEd school list](https://www.deped.gov.ph).

## College

**Cavite State University Naic** is in Barangay Bucana. It started as the Cavite College of Fisheries and became part of CvSU in 2001. Tuition at state universities is free for qualified students under RA 10931.

## Enrolling in a public school

DepEd announces the enrollment period every school year. Bring:

- [ ] PSA birth certificate (for Kindergarten and new learners)
- [ ] Report card (School Form 9) from the last school attended, for transferees
- [ ] Learner Reference Number (LRN), if the learner has one

Public schools may not refuse a learner who lacks a document at enrollment. Ask about the deadline to submit it instead.
