# Get first-time jobseeker documents for free

Under the First Time Jobseekers Assistance Act (RA 11261), you do not pay fees for the government documents you need to apply for your first job. Start with a certification from your barangay.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who qualifies

Filipinos looking for a job for the first time, including fresh graduates, students looking for part-time work, and out-of-school youth. You must have lived in your barangay for at least six months.

## Documents you can get for free (once)

Includes the barangay clearance, police clearance, NBI clearance, PSA birth and marriage certificates, medical certificate from a public hospital (laboratory fees not included), tax identification number (TIN), and the UMID or similar IDs. The free use applies once per document, within one year of your barangay certification.

## Requirements

- [ ] Proof that you have lived in the barangay for at least six months
- [ ] Valid ID or school ID
- [ ] Signed Oath of Undertaking, provided by the barangay

## Steps

1. Go to your barangay hall and ask for a Barangay Certification as a first-time jobseeker.
2. Sign the Oath of Undertaking.
3. Bring the certification when you request each document. Each agency keeps a copy and waives the fee.

## Fees

The certification and the covered documents are free.
