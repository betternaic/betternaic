# Apply for SPES

The Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES) gives students and out-of-school youth paid work during school breaks. Part of the pay comes from the employer and part from the government. In Naic, applications go through PESO.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can apply

- Ages 15 to 30
- Students, out-of-school youth who plan to go back to school, or dependents of displaced or soon-to-be displaced workers
- From a family with limited income, as set by DOLE's guidelines

## Requirements

- [ ] Accomplished SPES application form
- [ ] PSA birth certificate or other proof of age
- [ ] For students: latest grades or certificate of enrollment
- [ ] For out-of-school youth: certification from the barangay or school
- [ ] Proof of family income, such as the parents' income tax return or a certificate of indigency
- [ ] Recent photos (ask how many)

## Steps

1. Watch for the SPES call for applicants from PESO, usually before the summer break.
2. Submit your application and documents at PESO in the Naic Municipal Hall.
3. Wait for the screening and your assignment to a government office or private employer.
4. Work the agreed period and keep a record of your attendance.
5. Receive your pay. The government share may be released separately, sometimes as an education voucher.

## Fees

Applying is free.
