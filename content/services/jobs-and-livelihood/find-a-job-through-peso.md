# Find a job through PESO

The Public Employment Service Office (PESO) of Naic helps jobseekers find work for free. It matches you with employers, refers you to openings, and runs or announces job fairs.

> Confirm before you go: requirements and fees can change. Check with the office or the [Offices page](/government/offices).

## Who can use it

Anyone looking for work, including fresh graduates, out-of-school youth, returning OFWs, and people who lost their jobs.

## What to bring

- [ ] Resume or bio-data, with a recent photo
- [ ] Valid ID
- [ ] Copies of diplomas, transcripts or training certificates
- [ ] Clearances employers often ask for (barangay, police or NBI). First-time jobseekers can get these for free.

## Steps

1. Register at PESO at the Naic Municipal Hall. You can also create a profile on [PhilJobNet](https://philjobnet.gov.ph), the government's online job portal.
2. Tell the PESO staff the kind of work you want and your skills.
3. Get referrals to employers with openings.
4. Watch for job fair announcements, and bring several copies of your resume.

## Other help at PESO

- Career guidance and employment coaching
- Referrals to skills training and TESDA courses
- The Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES)
- Help for returning OFWs

## Fees

PESO services are free. Never pay a placement fee for a local job offered through PESO.
